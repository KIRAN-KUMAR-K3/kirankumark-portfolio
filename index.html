Physical Security Network Hardening

Situation

The institution's IP camera network, deployed campus-wide for physical security, had not been assessed for authentication and network-segmentation risk.

Challenge

Demonstrate real business risk clearly enough to secure stakeholder buy-in for remediation, without disrupting live physical-security operations.

Actions taken
  • Assessed authentication controls across the camera network
  • Ran an authorized proof-of-concept demonstration for stakeholders
  • Delivered a phased remediation plan: credential rotation, access controls, and network segmentation
  • Designed a segmented network architecture to isolate physical-security devices
Results
  • Authentication exposure fully remediated and confirmed secured by the infrastructure team
  • Physical-security network isolated on a dedicated segment (DMZ), reducing lateral-movement risk
